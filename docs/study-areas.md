# Rural study areas

The study uses five settlement-centered pilot areas. Quba, Lahıc and Slavyanka have been modeled. The other two areas were screened using the same OpenStreetMap extract and study radius. The areas were chosen to compare terrain and settlement patterns across different parts of Azerbaijan.

## Comparison design

Lahıc provides a second mountainous pilot with a substantially denser set of mapped settlement points. Slavyanka adds a western case in Gədəbəy, Piran adds a southern case in Lerik, and Bist adds a Nakhchivan case. [Lahıc reserve](https://rmc.tourismboard.az/az/managed-units/lahic-dovlet-tarix-medeniyyet-qorugu-60a2126d05578) · [Gədəbəy district listing](https://mia.gov.az/az/map/view/4/) · [Piran district listing](https://lankaran.edu.gov.az/az/page/18242) · [Ordubad district listing](https://ordubad-ih.gov.az/inzibati-erazi)

Keep the 17.5 km radius, 90 m elevation product, 20 m and 40 m masts, and 3 km and 6 km site grids for the first comparison. Check national boundaries before placing candidate sites or counting demand, particularly for the Ordubad case. Report each area's mapped settlement count, reachable share, minimum towers and estimated cost per settlement reached alongside totals. The five pilots support comparisons under the same modeling assumptions.
