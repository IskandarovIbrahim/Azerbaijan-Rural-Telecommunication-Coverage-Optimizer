## Comparison design

Lahıc provides a second mountainous pilot with a substantially denser set of mapped settlement points. Slavyanka adds a western case in Gədəbəy, Piran adds a southern case in Lerik, and Bist adds a Nakhchivan case. [Lahıc reserve](https://rmc.tourismboard.az/az/managed-units/lahic-dovlet-tarix-medeniyyet-qorugu-60a2126d05578) · [Gədəbəy district listing](https://mia.gov.az/az/map/view/4/) · [Piran district listing](https://lankaran.edu.gov.az/az/page/18242) · [Ordubad district listing](https://ordubad-ih.gov.az/inzibati-erazi)

All five areas use a 17.5 km study radius, the 90 m elevation product, 20 m and 40 m masts, and 3 km and 6 km candidate grids. The Bist study applies the national boundary to candidate and settlement points within the circle. [Bist study](bist-study.md)
